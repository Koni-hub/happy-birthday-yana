A note for you,
only you.

My Yana,

There are some things I want to say that don’t fit inside a birthday card — so I’m keeping them here, just for you.

Thank you for being the person I look for in every room. For the way you care so gently, even when you’re tired. For every little thing you do without realizing anyone notices. I notice. I always notice.

If I could give you one thing today, it would be the ability to see yourself the way I do: soft, strong, a little ridiculous, and so deeply worthy of every good thing.

This year, I hope you let yourself be loved as much as you love everyone else. And on the days you can’t quite believe in yourself, borrow my belief for a while — I have more than enough for both of us.

Happy birthday, my favorite person. I’m so glad the world has you, and even gladder that I get to love you.

Always,
your favorite person
